## 1.0.0
- First version: the aquarium holds up to 5 fish. Use the tank to open a list where you can take any fish out, add the fish you are holding, or swap it for one in the tank.
- Other players see your first two fish; fish 3 to 5 are shown only in your own game.
- Also makes the game's own aquarium fish show up for players who join after you (the game's own "resend when somebody joins" call fails with an error).
