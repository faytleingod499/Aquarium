## 1.1.0
- New: players who also have this mod now see your fish 3 to 5 in the aquarium, and you see theirs (shown while you are in the aquarium, up to 24 players and 60 fish at once). They are sent through Steam lobby data, so nothing is needed on the server.
- Players without the mod still see fish 1 and 2 only: the game refuses more than two aquarium fish from one player, and the mod can't change that for them.
- The list now says "players with this mod can see this fish" for places 3 to 5.

## 1.0.1
- Fixed: fish 2 to 5 turned into a copy of fish 1 after about 10 seconds. The game refreshes its aquarium fish from the first fish every 10 seconds, and the extra fish did the same. Each fish now keeps its own fish.
- The second fish (the other one players see) is re-sent every 10 seconds, like the game does for the first.

## 1.0.0
- First version: the aquarium holds up to 5 fish. Use the tank to open a list where you can take any fish out, add the fish you are holding, or swap it for one in the tank.
- Other players see your first two fish; fish 3 to 5 are shown only in your own game.
- Players who join after you see your fish right away (the game itself sends them within 10 seconds).
